# Uncertainty and multiple comparisons

Comparisons pair daily marked-equity P/L divided by initial capital on exactly matching pair/fold dates and cost/protection settings. Entry times/trade counts differ, so trade-level R samples are not falsely treated as paired independent observations. Moving-block bootstrap resamples adjacent two-trading-day blocks within each fold, with 2,000 deterministic replicates. Fewer than eight daily observations or a fold shorter than two days returns an undefined interval. These are unadjusted percentile intervals, not simultaneous family intervals; they are illustrative because short samples cannot establish block length, stationarity, exchangeability or coverage. Block sign-flip p-values likewise require unverified symmetry/block independence. Holm adjustments include the complete 1312-comparison family, including unavailable comparisons; no reliable advantage is claimed even if an exploratory adjusted value is small. No statistically reliable M15/H1/H4, confirmation or adaptive superiority follows from this archive. H4 warm-up gaps and low trade counts further limit interpretation. Full numerical intervals/p-values are in comparisons.json. Positive PF or a handful of wins cannot establish profitability.
