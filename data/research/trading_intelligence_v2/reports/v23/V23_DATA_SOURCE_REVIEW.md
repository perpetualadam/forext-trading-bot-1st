- DUKASCOPY: DATABASE_USE_PERMISSION_NOT_ESTABLISHED. Reviewed terms restrict constructing databases; do not assume another jurisdiction or a public URL grants this project rights. No authenticated service used. [Provider documentation](https://www.dukascopy.com/swiss/english/about/faq/?mob=0); [terms/evidence](https://www.dukascopy.com/europe/english/legal-pages/terms-of-use/).

No authenticated broker endpoint, protected-service scrape, account registration, legal-form submission or dependency installation was used. This is a project-specific permission gap, not a conclusion that these providers prohibit every form of research. The user selected the importer-only fallback. Future public acquisition needs independently verified provider identity, applicable rights permitting automated local research and derived-result publication, documented schema/timezone/completion semantics and a bounded retrieval plan. Suggested upper bounds for a separately reviewed workflow: one request at a time, at most one per second, 15-second timeout, one retry, 64 MiB/file and 512 MiB total, no redirects to authenticated services, no OANDA hosts. These bounds are proposals; no downloader is active.
