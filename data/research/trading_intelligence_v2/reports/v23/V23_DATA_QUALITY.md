# Importer and quality checks

Supported import schema: OBSERVED_M5_BID_ASK_MID_V1 CSV with columns time, instrument, complete, mid_o, mid_h, mid_l, mid_c, bid_o, bid_h, bid_l, bid_c, ask_o, ask_h, ask_l, ask_c. Every row needs literal true completion, an instrument matching the manifest, an aware start timestamp normalized to UTC, finite positive midpoint/bid/ask OHLC and uncrossed quotes. M5 alignment, strict ordering and duplicate rejection apply within and across files. Files are consumed in manifest order; disorder is never silently sorted or deduplicated. Malformed/extra/missing fields reject the import. Ask and midpoint OHLC must be supplied from observed quotes; bid-only minute files are not silently given assumed asks or synthetic midpoint extrema. Other source formats require a separately documented adapter.

Original decimal lexemes are preserved in original CSV files and hashed; replay uses the existing IEEE-754 Bar representation. No decimal rounding, interpolation or price repair. Off-instrument-grid precision is reported rather than silently rounded. Weekend-containing gaps are labelled; only explicit reviewed calendar windows qualify as documented holidays. Other gaps remain unexplained outage/unverified holiday. Observed market closure is not inferred from an invented holiday calendar. Full gap/coverage results are in outputs/v23/data_quality.json. D1 remains disabled because no reviewed daily-session calendar is supplied. Historical coverage was not expanded.
