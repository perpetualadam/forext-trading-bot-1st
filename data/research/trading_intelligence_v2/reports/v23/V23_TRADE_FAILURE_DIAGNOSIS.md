# Loss evidence and limitations

Every closed trade is saved with a stable dataset/spec/scenario-bound ID, price, direction, timeframe, strategy, causal entry H1/H4 context, regime/volatility, stop/target geometry, holding/exit, gross/net/risk/R/costs and source-quality information. Labels may coexist: negative gross outcome, costs overwhelming positive gross, spread > one quarter ATR, stop within one entry bar, stop inside one ATR, target >=4 ATR, entry against known higher trend, observed later context reversal, timeout/session/fold flatten, volatility/stagnation tags, breakout stop-out, protection giveback, gaps and undetermined causal contribution. They are hypotheses and observed conditions, not a causal allocation of each loss. Thresholds are preregistered diagnostics and never entry filters.

A losing gross exit can reflect direction, entry timing or exit rules. A wide target being reached rarely does not by itself establish it is too distant. Stop-placement causality requires a separately frozen counterfactual; none is optimised here. OHLC cannot establish intrabar excursion ordering: the exit bar contributes only to upper MFE/MAE bounds for intrabar exits; completed held bars and known execution endpoints supply lower bounds. Opening/data gaps have unknown upper path bounds. No exact MFE/MAE is fabricated from full exit-bar extrema. Post-entry context observations are explicitly post-trade diagnostics, not selection features. Detailed counts by policy are in failure_counts.json; sums of contributing tags can exceed the number of trades.
