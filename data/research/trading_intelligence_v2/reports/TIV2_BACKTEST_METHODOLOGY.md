# Replay methodology

All signal features use bar i-1, available at open i. Phases: current opening quotes, opening exits, entries, stop-first intrabar exits, closing quotes, time/protection decisions, MTM. Intrabar profits cannot fund earlier opening entries. BUY enters ask plus slippage and exits bid minus slippage; SELL reverses. Both stop and target touched => stop first. Adverse stop gaps fill at opening executable quote; favorable target gaps get no improvement. Missing-interval held exposure gets a conservative stop/open-worse fill; unpriceable conversions or end exposure invalidate the run. This conservative gap rule is a model assumption, not a known broker fill. Sizing includes worst planned stop loss, exit slippage and roundtrip commissions in account currency; gap losses can exceed planned risk.

USD research account 10,000; per-trade risk 0.25%, total planned risk 2%, six positions, two same-USD-direction slots, leverage 20, 80% margin capacity, stop=max(2 ATR,4 pips), target=2R, max hold 12 M5 bars. Daily flatten at 20:55 UTC and no subsequent entries until UTC midnight. The ATR geometry is a declared research assumption. A separate original-default geometry benchmark reuses trading.sl_tp_distance_for_entry with cleared environment (USE_ATR_STOPS defaults false, SL_FALLBACK_PIPS=20, TP_RISK_REWARD=2). This is a source-default benchmark, not verification of current production settings. It was added after the initial study to satisfy the original-geometry comparison, without fitting parameters; those comparisons remain exploratory. Base slippage 0.2 pip per side; stress 0.5. Commission 3 account units/100k per side; conservative 1bp notional/day funding on date crossing. These are declared assumptions, not retrieved broker terms. Fixed UTC sessions are descriptive and not DST-aware exchange calendars. FX conversion uses causal observed bid/ask graph: assets liquidated at bid/ask inverse and liabilities valued at adverse side. Quotes older than 300 seconds or from the future cannot be used. USD_JPY pip is 0.01; raw cross-currency units never summed.

Metrics: account-currency and R expectancy, PF, net/gross/cost, win/loss, exposure duration, consecutive losses, MTM drawdown and daily Sharpe/Sortino only with >=20 observations. Aggregated separate-fold returns divide by two independent 10,000 capitals; aggregate MTM drawdown/Sharpe undefined. Per-run MTM metrics remain available. OHLC cannot reveal tick sequence, liquidity or fill latency; this simulator makes explicit assumptions, not a broker execution guarantee.
