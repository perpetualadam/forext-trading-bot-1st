# R.67B Failure-mode analysis

Design review of the reconciliation and close machines against the R.66 findings. The current source still behaves as R.66 described. This table is the safe state a later implementation has to reach. It is not a patch. `REAL_NETWORK_EXECUTION_NOT_TESTED`. Production trading remains NO-GO.

Severity uses the same scale as the R.66 register. None of these modes were observed on a live account. Detection is from durable state and reads, not from a log line alone.

| Case | Severity | Detection | Safe state | Recovery | Residual risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate close | High. Current source can submit again after a timeout (`oanda_exec.py` 431–433, next manage cycle after `bot_loop.py` 1073–1083, `R66-11`). | A second `CLOSE_SUBMITTING` for the same account, instrument, and book side, or two fill transactions for one `close_operation_id`. | One operation in `CLOSE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` or a later classified state. The unique rule rejects the second insert. No HTTP call follows a rejected insert. | Read `TradeDetails` and closing transactions. Classify partial or full. Do not send the duplicate. A human may still use the broker's own controls. | If the first call left the process before the pre-send commit, the design has no broker idempotency key to cancel it. That window is closed only by committing `CLOSE_SUBMITTING` before bytes. Server-side deduplication is `NOT_VERIFIED` and is not assumed. |
| Partial close | High. A non-raising `PositionClose` drops the whole local row (`oanda_exec.py` 450, `bot_loop.py` 1084, `R66-10`). | `tradesClosed` sum is greater than zero and less than the grant, or a later read shows fewer units. | `CLOSE_PARTIALLY_FILLED`. Parent observation remains. `broker_units` updates only from a read. | Record the child reduction. Re-read protection on the remaining trade ids. A further close requires a new grant for the remainder. | Allocation of one `PositionClose` across several trades is `NOT_VERIFIED` when `tradesClosed` is absent. That absence stays `CLOSE_RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`, so the remainder can be understated until a read. |
| Unknown close outcome | High (`R66-11`). | Timeout, HTTP 408, 5xx, empty body, or `CLOSE_SUBMITTING` found at restart. | `CLOSE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. Provenance `CLOSE_AMBIGUOUS`. Units unchanged. Execution authority empty after restart. | Resolve only by trade and transaction reads. Failed reads stay unknown. | A fill can exist at the broker while the bot waits. The bot will not correct it by sending again. A person can. The bot will not block that person. |
| Broker position incorrectly marked flat | High. A successful snapshot that omits a symbol is worded as broker net 0 (`reconciliation.py` 771–774). Auto-fix can then treat a backed local row as disappeared (`603–628`). | `BROKER_FLAT_CONFIRMED` without a closing transaction id, or flat entered from a failed or incomplete read. | Previous observation plus `BROKER_STATE_STALE` or `BROKER_STATE_CONFLICT`. `LOCAL_RECORD_RETIRED` forbidden. | Keep the last complete units. Require `resolve_broker_exit`'s closed-trade bar (`broker_exit.py` 197–202) before flat. | A complete empty `positions` array can still be a true flat account. The design withholds retirement until each previously seen trade is confirmed, so a genuinely flat side stays tracked until those reads succeed. |
| Imported manual position closed without authority | High (`R66-04`). | Observation provenance `EXTERNAL_UNKNOWN` together with a `PositionClose` or a local delete. `execution_kind=reconcile_import` is the current label (`positions.py` 169) and is not a grant. | `OBSERVED_BROKER_POSITION` or `TRACKED_UNMANAGED`. `log_only` and broker mode do not enter `MANAGEMENT_AUTHORISED`. | Leave the broker position untouched. An operator `CLOSE` grant is required before any send. Weekend flatten and profit protection may write a request and must not send. | The current manage branch still calls `execute_trade` for a local row (`bot_loop.py` 1058–1084). For `reconcile_import` that call does not send `PositionClose` and can still delete the row. The design forbids that delete. The code still does it until a later stage. |
| Local fallback mislabelled protected | High (`R66-05`, `R66-03`). | `sl_source=local_fallback` or a price with no `broker_stop_order_id` stored as `BROKER_PROTECTED`. | `LOCAL_REFERENCE_ONLY` or `PROTECTION_UNKNOWN`. `protection_missing` is false unless the state is `UNPROTECTED_CONFIRMED`. | Keep the reference for display. Do not send a stop. A later `TradeDetails` read in this process is the only entry to `BROKER_PROTECTED`. | The current parser cannot store a stop order id (`reconciliation.py` 375–388), so it also cannot prove protection. `broker_sl_tp_hit` returning false avoids a local-price market close and does not create proof. Whether the live payload contains the id is `NOT_VERIFIED`. |
| Stale broker snapshot | Medium, rising to high if a close or a flat decision uses it. | `snapshot_id` older than `BROKER_SNAPSHOT_FRESHNESS`, or the latest read failed (`reconciliation.py` 737–741). | `BROKER_STATE_STALE`. Prior units retained. Protection `PROTECTION_STALE` or `PROTECTION_UNKNOWN`. Unconsumed `CLOSE` grants die on snapshot mismatch. | Wait for a complete read and a new epoch. Do not increment the epoch on failure. | `BROKER_SNAPSHOT_FRESHNESS` is unnamed, so a later stage must choose a window before implementation. Choosing it is a parameter change this stage does not make. |
| Concurrent reconcile and close | High (`R66-16`). | Observation version and close version change in overlapping transactions, or the RAM dict is written by both tasks. | Reconcile commits observation only. The pre-send commit is the only send permission. A snapshot change does not clear `pending_close_id`. | Loser of `WHERE version = N` re-reads and does not send. Flat retirement waits until the close operation is classified. | The current dict has no version. Until the additive records exist, the race remains in the source. This design does not hold a lock across the broker call, so a slow `PositionClose` can overlap a read. The overlap is safe only if the close row is already `CLOSE_SUBMITTING` and reconcile will not grant or retire. |
| Restart during close | High (`R66-11`, `R67A` invariant 10). | Process start finds `CLOSE_SUBMITTING` or `CLOSE_AUTHORISED`. | Execution authority empty. `CLOSE_SUBMITTING` becomes `CLOSE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. `CLOSE_AUTHORISED` is revoked. Positions do not reload as grants. Orders may reload (`app.py` 574–579) and still are not close grants. | Read-only resolution. A new close needs a new grant after classification. | A crash before the pre-send commit cannot be distinguished from a crash that never intended to send. Both refuse to send. A crash after bytes and before commit is unknown, which is the conservative side, and it can delay a legitimate second close until a read finishes. |
| Multiple trades on one instrument | High when blended (`reconciliation.py` 270–286 and 465–474 use the first trade id for `TradeDetails`). | One symbol row, a net of long and short, or protection taken from a single trade id while others are unread. | One observation per trade id. Both sides open: management forbidden. `PositionClose` evidence must list every affected `tradeID` in `tradesClosed` before full confirmation. | Unread trade ids stay `PROTECTION_UNKNOWN`. The side confidence is the worst id. No netting. | Hedging permission is `NOT_VERIFIED`. A host that rejects two sides would surface as a broker fact this design has not seen. The safe local rule is still to refuse management while both sides have units. |
| Loss of database connection | Medium for new actions, high if RAM is then treated as authority. | Commit failure, or `reconcile_metadata` not saved (`reconciliation.py` 844). | No new transition. No HTTP close. No retirement. Last committed version remains the book. RAM is cache. | When the database returns, recover unresolved close rows before management. Do not replay `CLOSE_SUBMITTING` as a send. | `broker_exit_pending` and `exec_orders` cannot store this machine. A later stage that writes only those tables would miss the invariant. The additive records are required. They are not created here. If the database dies after the broker accepts and before the evidence commit, the position can be flat at the broker while the bot still shows the previous units. That is preferred to a second close. |
| Conflicting broker evidence | High. | Fill says full and the next complete OpenPositions still shows units, or two trade reads disagree, or a cancel and a fill are both present. | Fill wins over a cancel in the same body. Across reads, `BROKER_STATE_CONFLICT` and `CLOSE_RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`. If the conflict remains, `CLOSE_MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. | Stop sending. Keep both snapshot ids. A person resolves the account in the broker's tools. The bot records a later consistent read and does not invent the outcome. | Transaction ordering between OpenPositions and TradeDetails is `NOT_VERIFIED`. Two reads can be separately complete and still differ by a fill that landed between them. The conflict state exists for that case. It can hold a side in manual review longer than a human expects. It will not trade out of that hold by itself. |

## Modes the design does not open

- New entries stay refused (`R66-01`). No failure mode above is a reason to return `continue_existing_production_broker`.
- Missing protection does not create a stop order (`R66-05`).
- `R66-02`, `R66-07`, `R66-08`, and `R66-15` stay out of this machine. They sit on the unreachable entry tail or on sizing. This design does not make that tail reachable.
- Research runners from R.62–R.65 are not part of recovery.

## What remains true in the working tree

The safe states above are not what the source does today. Import can still create a manageable row under broker mode while `log_only` is set (`R66-04`). A live-kind close can still drop the local row without closed units (`R66-10`). A timeout can still be followed by another close (`R66-11`). Those are residual risks of the unchanged program, recorded so this design is not mistaken for a fix.
