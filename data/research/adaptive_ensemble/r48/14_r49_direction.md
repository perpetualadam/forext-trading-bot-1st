# Recommended R.49 direction

Do not start R.49 from this stage.

Authenticated account evidence was not obtained. R.49 must not be a trading stage, a candle download, or a repair of the USD path. It also must not start by pasting a token or an account identifier into chat.

A later stage can use the R.48 gate only after a separate instruction approves a protected way to supply the credential to that isolated process. That instruction has to forbid order endpoints, forbid logging the credential, and forbid storing the account identifier. Until it exists, the gate stays closed.

Leave the R.42 range rule unchanged. Leave `MAX_POSITION_UNITS` unchanged.
