# Broker-token permission

The permission on a real OANDA token remains `UNKNOWN`.

This adapter refuses order paths and refuses to call the real HTTPS class. That is a property of this research process. It is not a property of a token issued by OANDA. Nothing reviewed in R.45 through R.51 shows that a token which can call these two GET routes is prevented from creating or changing orders.

A future read-only claim needs evidence from the broker that the credential itself cannot trade. Until that evidence exists, the token is treated as capable of placing trades.

No real token was read or tested in R.51.
