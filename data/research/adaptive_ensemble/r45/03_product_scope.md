# R.45 product and sub-account scope

Public availability is not permission for this account. Nothing below classifies the user as retail, professional, spread-bet, CFD, v20, or OANDA One.

| Topic | Published statement | Scope limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal entity | The UK legal index is titled OANDA Europe LTD. The spreads page names OANDA (Europe) Limited and the FCA retail leverage cap. The help page says the viewer is on OANDA Europe Limited. | Entity names are not an account contract. |
| Spread betting | A spread-betting sub-account is offered. The customer does not own the underlying. Long means a wager that price rises. Short means a wager that it falls. Profit requires a move beyond the spread. | The same pages also describe CFDs. A CFD example is not a spread bet. |
| CFDs | CFD trading exchanges the difference in price. The retail risk warning, 76.6% of retail CFD accounts lose money, is a CFD warning. | That percentage is not a spread-bet result and is not a research outcome. |
| Platforms | Spread betting page: OANDA web, iOS, Android, TradingView, and MT4. It states you cannot spread bet using MT5. | MT5 prices on the spreads page are therefore not spread-bet prices. |
| Hours, marketing | Spread betting page: five days, 22:00 GMT Sunday to 22:00 GMT Friday. Hours FAQ: approximately 22:00 Sunday to 22:00 Friday GMT, subject to daylight saving and holidays. | The detailed FX session is published under "Forex CFDs", New York 17:05 Sunday to 16:59 Friday, with a six-minute break. |
| v20 | Margin uses current mid prices. Margin close-out percent runs up to 100%, then all open positions are liquidated and contingent orders on them are cancelled. Financing is credited or debited daily, usually at 5pm ET. | This is a sub-account type, not proof this project uses v20. |
| OANDA One | Margin uses the sided price at order time and stays constant in home-currency units. Coverage percent runs down to 50%, then the largest losing trade is closed, repeatedly. For most instruments, financing accrues in unrealised P&L until the position closes. Commodities are debited daily. | Not proof this project uses OANDA One. |
| Retail versus professional | Retail major forex is published as 30:1 and 3.33% margin. Non-major forex and gold are 20:1 and 5%. Professional major forex is published as 1.0% and 100:1. The page says professional leverage is set by OANDA and the FCA retail cap does not apply. | Professional terms are not this account unless a later check shows that category. |
| Home currency | Examples include GBP-denominated sub-accounts. The charges page applies conversion when the instrument quote is not the account home currency. | A GBP example is not this account's currency. |
| Tax | The spread-betting page says UK spread-betting profits are exempt from UK capital gains tax and stamp duty, and that tax law and individual circumstances vary. | No tax conclusion is made for the user. |

The spreads page FAQ says to watch "margin closeout percent" and that closer to 100% means closer to closeout. That matches the v20 definition. OANDA One uses a coverage percentage that closes at 50% or below. The two descriptions are both on the public site and are not the same dial. A future spec has to name the sub-account before it picks one.
