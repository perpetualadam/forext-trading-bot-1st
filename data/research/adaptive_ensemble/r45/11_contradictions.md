## Contradictions and scope clashes

- The GSLO page says guaranteed stops are available on CFDs. The spread-betting page says they are available on forex, indices, and gold for that product. Both stand. Neither gives a forex premium.
- v20 closeout is a percent rising to 100% and a full liquidation. OANDA One is a coverage percent falling to 50% and a one-trade liquidation. The FAQ's "closer to 100%" sentence matches v20 only.
- Financing conversion in the worked example uses 0.5%. Settlement conversion on the charges page uses 1.0% and is limited to v20 and v20 MT4. These are different sentences, not one fee.
- Spread-bet marketing hours are 22:00 GMT Sunday to 22:00 GMT Friday. The FX session table is New York 17:05 to 16:59 under Forex CFDs, with a six-minute break. Daylight saving can move the clock. They are not silently treated as the same session.
- The spreads page takes indicative prices from MT5. The spread-betting page says MT5 cannot place a spread bet.
- In one v20 worked example the narrative formula gives a negative margin available, while the summary table on the same example prints that amount without the minus sign. The formula text is the one used here. The table cell is not treated as a second rule.

No production or research calculation was changed to pick a side.
