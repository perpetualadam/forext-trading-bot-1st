# R.45 spreads, charges, and GBP conversion

## Spreads

The spread is the difference between the buy and sell price. The spreads page says spreads for CFD trading or spread betting can be wider at the open, the close, data releases, and geopolitical events, and that a fixed spread may be imposed. Weekend and reopen gaps can trigger stops or closeouts. Prices on that page are sourced from the MT5 platform and are indicative. The spread-betting page says MT5 cannot be used to spread bet. An MT5 indication is not a spread-bet executable price.

"From 0.6 pips" on EUR/USD is a marketing floor on the spread-betting page. It is not a historical bid/ask and it is not a fill.

## Commission

The spread-betting page says forex spread betting from one account is commission free. The charges page lists percentage commissions for share CFDs and ETF CFDs only. Those percentages are not forex spread-bet charges. No forex spread-bet commission schedule, other than the commission-free marketing sentence, was on the charges page.

## Other cash charges

The charges page says OANDA does not charge deposit or withdrawal fees, and that a bank might. Inactivity is up to £20 per client per month after 12 months without trading, not charged while a position is open, converted into the sub-account home currency, and not taken below zero. A GSLO premium is charged only if the guaranteed stop is triggered. The premium amount for the six pairs was not published.

## Conversion

When the instrument quote is not the account home currency, realised profit, loss, adjustments, fees, and charges are converted at the prevailing rate. The charges page says the rate is the mid price plus or minus 1.0%, and that this fee applies only on v20 and v20 MT4 sub-accounts. A profit is converted on the side that sells the profit currency. A loss uses the side that buys it. The worked GBP example converts USD profit on a EUR/USD trade through GBP/USD.

The financing page's worked example uses 0.5% for the financing conversion. That is a different published percentage from the 1.0% settlement fee. OANDA One is outside the sentence that limits the 1.0% fee to v20 and v20 MT4. OANDA One conversion is therefore not settled by that sentence.

## R.44 conflict

The production path still approximates USD. The shadow path still divides by GBP/USD or by 1.35. The public pages describe a home-currency conversion with a stated markup, and they show GBP only as an example. They do not say this account's home currency is GBP, and they do not define a spread-bet stake. The conflict is not repaired. A public indicative spread is not a historical executable spread. The shadow fallback of 1.35 does not appear in these pages.
