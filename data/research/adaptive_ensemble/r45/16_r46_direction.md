# Recommended R.46 direction

Do not start R.46 from this stage.

The public pages are enough to write a later specification only after the account type is known. They are not enough to choose v20 or OANDA One, to value a pip in GBP, or to treat the six pairs as authorised spread bets.

If a later stage is authorised, keep it to one route: either a redacted product sheet supplied by the user, or a separately approved read-only account check with a no-order rule. Do not download candles, do not insert the 0.6 pip phrase or the 3.33% class cap into a backtest, and do not repair the USD-versus-GBP code conflict in that stage.

Leave the R.42 range rule unchanged.
