# Recommended R.43 direction

Do not start R.43 from this stage.

The frozen reach rule confirmed one range in the research year, on GBP_USD H1, and that range was replaced by a newer level on the confirmation candle. H4 confirmed none. Searching a wider touch tolerance to create more ranges would be parameter fitting against the same exposed year. Leave the tolerance unchanged.

R.43, if later authorised, should stay descriptive or should wait for bid/ask data outside September 2025 through August 2026 before any economic question. It should not backtest these labels on the exposed year, rank instruments, or turn a confirmed range into an entry.

Expanded currency pairs and an adaptive H4/H1/M5 selector remain separate later questions. They are not part of this recommendation and they are not authorised here.
